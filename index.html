HOW_IT_WORKS.EXE

Earthlight FM turns NASA's night lights into music. Here's how to tune in:

  1. Pick a placeClick a continent on the map, or press World.
  2. Pick a timeWhat changed (2012 → 2023), one year, or two years to compare.
  3. Press PlayThe region is read from west to east, one note per slice.
  4. Watch the screensThe globe, graph, notes and log follow the music.
  5. Press PrintGet a field report explaining what you heard.

What you hear

  • Kalimba: more light, or brighter
  • Stylophone: less light, or dimmer
  • Silence: no lights, or no real change
  • Higher, louder notes mean a stronger signal.
